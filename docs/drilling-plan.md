# Drilling Plan

Status: Blocked draft for Milestone 10. Do not drill the original yet.

Drilling the original horse is irreversible. This plan is a gate, not permission.

## Entry Criteria

- Full-object and nostril scans are complete.
- Nostril test section exists.
- Nostril Nozzle prototype has passed fit and pour tests.
- Tube material and outer diameter are known.
- Drill path and target diameter are documented from test-piece results.
- Adam explicitly accepts the risk of modifying the original sculpture.

## Safety Notes

- Unknown material and coating may create unsafe dust.
- Use eye protection and respiratory protection appropriate to the material once
  identified.
- Keep alcohol, liquids, power supplies, and electronics away from drilling.
- Clamp or support the object without damaging finish.
- Stop after each step to inspect.

## Draft Sequence

1. Photograph the original nostril area before masking.
2. Mask the finish around the drill area.
3. Mark the drill path from validated test-piece geometry.
4. Drill a pilot hole.
5. Step up gradually to the validated size.
6. Inspect after every step.
7. Fit the Nostril Nozzle without forcing it.
8. Route tubes.
9. Test with water only.
10. Document results and damage, including any failure.
