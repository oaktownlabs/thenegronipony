# Calibration Harness Plan

Status: Draft for Milestone 2.

The bench harness validates the pumps before the sculpture gets involved. The
goal is a repeatable test rig that makes failures visible and data boring in the
best possible way.

## Bench Layout

- Bottles or temporary reservoirs on the left.
- Intake tubes routed cleanly to three pumps.
- Pumps fixed to a temporary deck so vibration does not move the tube path.
- Outlet tubes aimed into a measured cup or container on a scale.
- Controller, drivers, and power kept outside the splash zone.
- Paper towels and spill tray under the wet path.

## Tools and Parts To Confirm

- Pump candidates.
- Food/alcohol-safe tubing candidates.
- Scale resolution and max weight.
- Test vessels with stable bases.
- Spill tray.
- Label tape for pump and tube identity.
- Flush liquid and waste container.
- Camera or phone mount for repeatable media capture.

## Test Sets

### Baseline Flow

Run water through each pump at several durations to estimate mL per second.

### Repeatability

Repeat the same target volume multiple times without changing the physical
setup. Record every trial.

### Anti-Drip

Add reverse pump motion after dispense and compare visible dripping, measured
delivered volume, and air ingestion.

### Flush

Run flush liquid through each path after ingredient tests. Record volume,
duration, visible residue, and whether any aroma or color remains.

## Media Shot List

- Wide bench overview before testing.
- Close shot of tube routing.
- Scale readout during a dispense.
- Pump close-up with labels visible.
- Anti-drip success and failure clips.
- Calibration chart screen capture after data is processed.
