# Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation | Status |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| R-001 | Original horse sculpture is damaged during modification. | High | Medium | Photograph, measure, scan, print/test nostril section, and drill test pieces before touching the original. | Open |
| R-002 | Food-contact path uses unsafe tubing, fittings, or materials. | High | Medium | Verify alcohol compatibility and food-contact suitability before ingredient tests. Document all material choices. | Open |
| R-003 | Pump flow rates vary by liquid, tubing, voltage, or time. | Medium | High | Capture calibration data per pump/liquid setup and store constants in source control. | Open |
| R-004 | Leaks or drips damage electronics, furniture, or the sculpture finish. | High | Medium | Keep pumps/electronics serviceable in the sidecar, test with water first, add drip checks, and design clear fluid routing. | Open |
| R-005 | Sidecar becomes visually messy or hard to service. | Medium | Medium | Separate sculpture magic from machine serviceability. Keep bottles, pumps, power, and electronics in the sidecar. | Open |
| R-006 | Public launch lacks enough media to explain the build. | Medium | Medium | Capture photos, video, notes, failures, calibration data, and decisions throughout private build. | Open |
| R-007 | Unknown electrical loads make battery power unreliable. | Medium | Medium | Use plug-in power for V1 and treat battery as a later upgrade. | Open |
| R-008 | Alcohol dispensing is mistaken for commercial service readiness. | High | Low | State V1 non-goals clearly: prototype art machine, no public alcohol service or commercial bar deployment. | Open |
| R-009 | A pump remains energized or restarts after reset, E-stop, serial loss, browser failure, or invalid command. | High | Medium | Hardware emergency stop cuts pump power and clamps Kamoer SP low, deliberate re-arm, reset pull-downs, bounded firmware commands, local watchdog, maximum mass/time guards, and no automatic resume. Prove each fault path dry before wet tests. | Open |
| R-010 | Scale readings are biased by pump vibration, tube force, jet force, corner loading, drift, or wiring noise. | High | High | Independent scale sub-base, fixed outlet with air gap/slack loop, reference-mass qualification, off-center and drift checks, raw ADC retention, robust fit windows, and one-pump-at-a-time primary tests. | Open |
| R-011 | Network loss, object restart, or retry drops, reorders, or duplicates calibration samples. | High | Medium | Global device/boot/sequence identity plus event hash, IndexedDB spool-before-upload, alarm-drained outbox, D1 idempotency, contiguous published frontier, projected acknowledgments, gap reporting, and crash/replay tests. | Open |
| R-012 | A public viewer, stale producer, or leaked client value can mutate trials or control hardware. | High | Low | No remote pump-control API, no write secret in the SPA, Cloudflare Access on operator routes, Worker-side JWT validation, hashed expiring producer lease, exact-origin checks, and public read-only routes. | Open |
| R-013 | Simulated prototype values are mistaken for measured pump performance. | High | Medium | Production has honest empty states; simulation is isolated and persistently labelled; published curves require source trials and review status. | Open |
| R-014 | Ethanol vapor or a spill reaches exposed power electronics or the Gikfun brushed motor. | High | Medium | Open prototype is water-only; keep electronics outside the wet zone; require enclosure, food-path, vapor, and fire review before ingredient tests. | Open |
| R-015 | A pull-request preview writes production/staging calibration data or receives production secrets. | High | Medium | Remote PR previews are read-only; writable tests use local bindings or an explicitly isolated staging deployment; CI rejects production IDs, audiences, or secrets outside production. | Open |
| R-016 | Verbose serial telemetry blocks firmware or loses 80 SPS transient samples. | High | Medium | Use compact sample blocks at 460800 baud, keep UI rendering decoupled from storage, qualify device clock, and require a worst-case loss-free soak before physical transient trials. | Open |
