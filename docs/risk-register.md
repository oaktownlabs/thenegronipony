# Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation | Status |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| R-001 | Original horse sculpture is damaged during modification. | High | Medium | Photograph, measure, scan, print/test nostril section, and drill test pieces before touching the original. | Open |
| R-002 | Food-contact path uses unsafe tubing, fittings, or materials. | High | Medium | Verify alcohol compatibility and food-contact suitability before ingredient tests. Document all material choices. | Open |
| R-003 | Pump flow rates vary by liquid, tubing, voltage, or time. | Medium | High | Capture calibration data per pump/liquid setup and store constants in source control. | Open |
| R-004 | Leaks or drips damage electronics, furniture, or the sculpture finish. | High | Medium | Keep pumps/electronics serviceable in the sidecar, test with water first, add drip checks, and design clear fluid routing. | Open |
| R-005 | Sidecar becomes visually messy or hard to service. | Medium | Medium | Separate sculpture magic from machine serviceability. Keep bottles, pumps, power, and electronics in the sidecar. | Open |
| R-006 | Public launch lacks enough media to explain the build. | Medium | Medium | Capture photos, video, notes, failures, calibration data, and decisions throughout private build. | Open |
| R-007 | Unknown electrical loads make battery power unreliable. | Medium | Medium | Use plug-in power for V1 and treat battery as a later upgrade. | Open |
| R-008 | Alcohol dispensing is mistaken for commercial service readiness. | High | Low | State V1 non-goals clearly: prototype art machine, no public alcohol service or commercial bar deployment. | Open |
