# Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation | Status |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| R-001 | Original horse sculpture is damaged during modification. | High | Medium | Photograph, measure, scan, print/test nostril section, and drill test pieces before touching the original. | Open |
| R-002 | Food-contact path uses unsafe tubing, fittings, or materials. | High | Medium | Verify alcohol compatibility and food-contact suitability before ingredient tests. Document all material choices. | Open |
| R-003 | Pump flow rates vary by liquid, tubing, voltage, or time. | Medium | High | Capture calibration data per pump/liquid setup and store constants in source control. | Open |
| R-004 | Leaks or drips damage electronics, furniture, or the sculpture finish. | High | Medium | Keep pumps/electronics serviceable in the sidecar, test with water first, add drip checks, and design clear fluid routing. | Open |
| R-005 | Sidecar becomes visually messy or hard to service. | Medium | Medium | Separate sculpture magic from machine serviceability. Keep bottles, pumps, power, and electronics in the sidecar. | Open |
| R-006 | Public launch lacks enough media to explain the build. | Medium | Medium | Capture photos, video, notes, failures, calibration data, and decisions throughout private build. | Open |
| R-007 | Unknown electrical loads make battery power unreliable. | Medium | Medium | Use plug-in power for V1 and treat battery as a later upgrade. | Open |
| R-008 | Alcohol dispensing is mistaken for commercial service readiness. | High | Low | State V1 non-goals clearly: prototype art machine, no public alcohol service or commercial bar deployment. | Open |
| R-009 | A pump remains energized or restarts after reset, E-stop, serial loss, browser failure, invalid command, or a main loop wedged HIGH/LOW. | High | Medium | E-stop removes K1/K2 coil feed; K1 gates commands; K2 disconnects pump power; D8 main-loop edges retrigger an external `CD74HC123E`; pull-downs, physical re-arm, bounded commands, MCU watchdog, hard time guard, and calibrated mass guard add layers. Prove E-stop, USB loss, reset, D8 HIGH/LOW stall, broken wire, and fault paths dry before wet tests. Treat the custom circuit as fail-off prototype engineering, not safety certification. | Open |
| R-010 | Scale readings are biased by pump vibration, tube force, jet force, corner loading, drift, or wiring noise. | High | High | Independent scale sub-base, fixed outlet with air gap/slack loop, reference-mass qualification, off-center and drift checks, raw ADC retention, robust fit windows, and one-pump-at-a-time primary tests. | Open |
| R-011 | Network loss, object restart, or retry drops, reorders, or duplicates calibration samples. | High | Medium | Provisioned device ID plus EEPROM reset-session counter and sequence identity, host epoch handling after EEPROM erase/reflash, event hash, IndexedDB spool-before-upload, alarm-drained outbox, D1 idempotency, contiguous published frontier, projected acknowledgments, gap reporting, and crash/replay tests. | Open |
| R-012 | A public viewer, stale producer, or leaked client value can mutate trials or control hardware. | High | Low | No remote pump-control API, no write secret in the SPA, Cloudflare Access on operator routes, Worker-side JWT validation, hashed expiring producer lease, exact-origin checks, and public read-only routes. | Open |
| R-013 | Simulated prototype values are mistaken for measured pump performance. | High | Medium | Production has honest empty states; simulation is isolated and persistently labelled; published curves require source trials and review status. | Open |
| R-014 | Ethanol vapor or a spill reaches exposed power electronics or the Gikfun brushed motor. | High | Medium | Open prototype is water-only; keep electronics outside the wet zone; require enclosure, food-path, vapor, and fire review before ingredient tests. | Open |
| R-015 | A pull-request preview writes production/staging calibration data or receives production secrets. | High | Medium | Remote PR previews are read-only; writable tests use local bindings or an explicitly isolated staging deployment; CI rejects production IDs, audiences, or secrets outside production. | Open |
| R-016 | Serial telemetry blocks the 2 KB-SRAM firmware or loses samples. | High | Medium | Use fixed small buffers and compact frames at exact-divider 250000 baud; forbid `String`/dynamic JSON; keep UI rendering decoupled from storage; qualify device clock; prove 10 SPS loss-free first and require a separate worst-case soak before any physically verified 80 SPS mode. | Open |
| R-017 | An AVR library, buffer, or timer change silently breaks 20 kHz control, timing, or stack margin. | High | Medium | Reserve Timer0 for time and Timer1 for D9/D10 PWM, prohibit incompatible Timer1 libraries, host-test pure logic, compile for `arduino:avr:uno`, review flash/SRAM reports, and scope both outputs on the received board. | Open |
| R-018 | Marketplace claims for the WANPTEK supply are mistaken for certification, isolation, or transient-current evidence. | High | Medium | Inspect the received rating/earth label and input revision; measure negative-to-earth and 12 V behavior; use CC deliberately; capture startup current with a separate instrument; retain downstream E-stop and branch fuses. | Open |
| R-019 | The high-flow pump overfills or tips the nominal 100 mL catch cylinder before browser/cloud action can stop it. | High | Medium | Measure a conservative vessel working line and mass cutoff, keep stop timing local to calibrated firmware, impose a hard bounded run time, use an overload stop and wet tray, and prime to waste before collection. | Open |
| R-020 | Uncalibrated raw HX711 counts are presented as mass or a `maximumMassMg` command is accepted without an enforceable conversion. | High | Medium | Preserve raw counts, emit null mass until a reviewed calibration is provisioned, reject physical runs while scale calibration is absent, and test this fail-closed path. | Open |
| R-021 | The 10 SPS stream retains a finished trial ID forever, so the browser spool never drains and the trial cannot seal. | High | Medium | Emit one context-bearing complete/fault/stop transition, then make all later samples anonymous (`trial`/`step` null, pump none, duty/timer zero). Validate the exact fixture tail and ignore anonymous post-terminal samples during cloud sealing. | Open |
