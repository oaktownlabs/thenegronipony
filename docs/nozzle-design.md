# Nostril Nozzle Design Plan

Status: Draft for Milestone 7.

The Nostril Nozzle is the removable interface that holds three outlet tube ends
at the horse nostril. It should make cleaning and replacement simple while
avoiding irreversible sculpture changes until scan and test-piece work are done.

## V1 Design Intent

- Hold three separate outlet tubes.
- Avoid an internal mixing manifold.
- Remove for cleaning or tube replacement.
- Fit the nostril geometry after scan-derived testing.
- Keep tube ends inspectable.

## Unknowns

- Nostril geometry and wall thickness.
- Nozzle material.
- Tube outer diameter.
- Whether tube ends should sit flush or protrude.
- Retention method.
- Drill diameter and path.

## Prototype Sequence

1. Use the Milestone 4 scan to extract a nostril-region test piece.
2. Design a first nozzle around actual geometry.
3. Print or fabricate nozzle and test section.
4. Drill only the printed/test section.
5. Fit tubes and run water/red-liquid tests.
6. Update this file with geometry and fit results before the original is touched.
