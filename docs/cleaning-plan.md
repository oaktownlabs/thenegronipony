# Cleaning Plan

Status: Draft for Milestone 11.

Cleaning must be straightforward enough to happen after a party and explicit
enough that future builders understand the food path.

## Draft Flow

1. Remove ingredient bottles.
2. Place intake tubes into flush liquid.
3. Run flush mode for each pump path.
4. Run air or storage-safe clearing step only if validated.
5. Remove and rinse the Nostril Nozzle.
6. Wipe the lake/base area.
7. Inspect for sticky residue, color, aroma, leaks, and tube damage.

## Unknowns

- Approved flush liquid.
- Tube material and replacement interval.
- Whether any ingredients leave persistent color or aroma.
- Storage position for tubes between uses.
- Whether the Nostril Nozzle can be dishwasher-safe; do not assume.
