# Sidecar Design Plan

Status: Draft for Milestone 8.

The sidecar is the tasteful lab-equipment half of The Negroni Pony. It should
show the bottles and pumps clearly, keep wet paths serviceable, and keep power
electronics protected from spills.

## Functional Zones

- Three upright 750 mL bottle positions.
- Intake tube routing from bottles to pumps.
- Three peristaltic pump mounts.
- Outlet tube routing into the umbilical.
- Electronics bay.
- Power inlet and strain relief.
- Optional clear or smoked acrylic cover.
- Optional brass or Oaktown Labs maker's mark.

## Layout Principles

- Bottles visible and easy to replace.
- Pumps accessible for tube service.
- Electronics separated from wet paths.
- Tubes routed with gentle bends.
- Umbilical exits cleanly toward the horse/lake module.
- Labels remain readable for calibration and debugging.

## Unknowns

- Pump dimensions and mounting holes.
- Bottle holder approach.
- Sidecar footprint.
- Material stack: wood, acrylic, aluminum, printed brackets, or hybrid.
- Umbilical connector and strain relief.
- Power supply enclosure requirements.

## Prototype Sequence

1. Confirm pump and bottle dimensions.
2. Sketch bottle/pump/electronics positions.
3. Mock layout with cardboard, foam board, or temporary deck.
4. Confirm tube bend radius and service access.
5. Draft CAD/laser files.
6. Fabricate first sidecar.
7. Mount pumps and bottles.
8. Run water test before ingredient testing.
